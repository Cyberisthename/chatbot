# Discoveries Compendium

This is the evidence-indexed record of the team's findings in AI, quantum-inspired computation, and structure-first language analysis.  Every item names its tag and a repository file that contains the stated result. **All quantum results are exact classical computations/simulations, not hardware results.** “Structure” never means decipherment or meaning.

The six proposed training directions, their pre-registration requirements, and their failure criteria are collected in [QUANTUM_AI_TRAINING_IDEAS.md](QUANTUM_AI_TRAINING_IDEAS.md); this compendium links to that source rather than repeating them.

## Proven

- **[measured] Deterministic FBSC reconstruction is exact for its own generated family.** Two fresh instances gave `MSE = 0.0` and `max|ΔA| = 0.0` in **22/22** grid cells (`d=2`, `n=8…1024`; `d=3,4`, `n=8…512`). At `n=1024,d=2`, the stored output is **16,384 B**, the dense ambient count is about `10^309.8 B`, and regeneration was **0.21 s** in the reported run. The family has at most three free continuous seed parameters, so it cannot represent generic states; at `n=8`, the searched best fit to a Haar-random 256-dimensional control was MSE **1.766**, while the own-state control was **0.000e+00**. **Evidence:** [ERROR_BOUND_PROOF.md](validation/ERROR_BOUND_PROOF.md) §§2–6 and [error_bound_verify.json](validation/error_bound_verify.json).

- **[measured] The NISQ comparison juxtaposes an owned exact simulation with explicitly analytic noise curves.** At `n=32,D=10`, the three model fidelities are **0.004**, **0.053**, and **0.376**; their first depths below 50% are **1**, **1**, and **6**. The owned d=4,n=16 row gives MSE **0**, barrier **100.7657 kJ/mol**, and ambient dimension `4^16=2^32`. **[interpretation]** This is a depth/noise-axis comparison, not hardware measurement or general quantum advantage. **Evidence:** [VALIDATION_BENCHMARK.md](validation/VALIDATION_BENCHMARK.md) §§2–4 and [validate_benchmark.json](validation/validate_benchmark.json).

- **[measured] The single-excitation embedding of the core family is exactly an MPS of bond dimension 2.** Two independent constructions gave fidelity **1.000000** at `n=4,8,12,16,20`; sampled amplitudes at `n=32…1024` had worst error **0.0**. The separate braid-state family is not similarly low-rank: its exact MPS bond grew **16→32→64→128** at `n=8,10,12,14`; the owned TT-SVD reproduced the approved sweep with `max|Δfid| = 0.0`. **Evidence:** [TN_CAPSTONE_REPORT.md](validation/tn_capstone/TN_CAPSTONE_REPORT.md) §§3–6 and [TN_CAPSTONE_results.json](validation/tn_capstone/TN_CAPSTONE_results.json).

- **[measured] Computed FBSC statistics can be explicitly excluded from every local-hidden-variable model in selected d=2 cases.** Brute force over **16/81/256** deterministic strategies establishes CGLMP's LHV maximum as **2.000000** for `d=2/3/4`. The approved qubit braid has CHSH **2.8250** at `n=2,3` and **2.0351** at `n=4`, all LP-infeasible; d=2 qudit-braid and core rows give **2.3449** and **2.0305**. No `d=3,4` violation was found at the searched settings. This is a statement about computed statistics, not hardware Bell tests. **Evidence:** [QUDIT_LHV_REPORT.md](artifacts/quantum_likeness/QUDIT_LHV_REPORT.md) §§1–4 and [qudit_lhv_results.json](artifacts/quantum_likeness/qudit_lhv_results.json).

- **[measured] Braid-space training loses to GD where the rung-1 objectives are differentiable.** With **800** evaluations each, GD/trainer losses were **0.000145/0.250391** (T0) and **0.137244/0.188340** (T1); T2 tied at **0.000000** for seed optimizer and trainer. On probe-native T3, where vanilla GD was N/A, trainer reached CHSH **2.663916** versus seed optimization **1.956037**, above the LHV bound **2.0**. **[interpretation]** The evidence supports braid-native optimization, not a general advantage over gradient descent. **Evidence:** [BRAID_VS_GD_BENCHMARK.md](artifacts/braid_vs_gd/BRAID_VS_GD_BENCHMARK.md) §§2–6 and [braid_vs_gd_results.json](artifacts/braid_vs_gd/braid_vs_gd_results.json).

- **[measured] A prior three-phase trainer found a protection/trainability trade-off.** Inserting **20** cancelling kinks changed braid weights by **0.0**; Gaussian float-weight noise at `σ=10^-3` changed the float matrix by **1.61×10^-3**. A 30% crossing-flip condition changed braid weights by **0.5** and had invariant distance **0.995**. Annealing ended at loss **0.064318**, while evolution and GD each reached **0.0**; the seed optimizer reached **0.053649**. **[interpretation]** This encoding protects a specific isotopy class but does not make optimization easier. **Evidence:** [TRAINER_REPORT.md](artifacts/trainer/TRAINER_REPORT.md) §§2–6 and [trainer_results.json](artifacts/trainer/trainer_results.json).

- **[measured] The resonance monitor now measures frequency from samples rather than fabricating it from bits.** On 4-second, 250-Hz synthetic tones, spectral-radius estimates were **41.0157 Hz** for 41.02 Hz and **38.0000**, **40.0000**, and **35.0000 Hz** for their corresponding controls; the retired bit heuristic produced **38.0 Hz** for the clean 41.02-Hz carrier. Bits-only calls now return `f0=None`. **[interpretation]** This is a noise-gated signal measurement, not a sentience or biological-resonance finding. **Evidence:** [F0_SPECTRAL_RADIUS_UNIFICATION.md](resonance_fix/F0_SPECTRAL_RADIUS_UNIFICATION.md) §§2–6 and [resonance_noise_gate_results.json](resonance_fix/resonance_noise_gate_results.json).

- **[measured] The Indus rebuild reproduces the committed subset's structural figures.** In **179** sides / **1,003** tokens, P385 opens **29/179** inscriptions (`z=9.84`); P385→P122 is **13.31×** enriched (`z=18.83`); `H1=6.2859`, `H2|H1=2.3252` bits (−63.0%), and size-matched LZ `Δ=−10.12%` (`0.79988±0.00320` vs shuffle `0.88996±0.00469`). **[interpretation]** These are writing-structure signals only, not a decipherment. **Evidence:** [INDUS_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md](artifacts/trifecta/INDUS_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md) and [indus_structure_results.json](artifacts/trifecta/indus_structure_results.json).

- **[measured] The rebuilt cross-script comparison puts Indus close to one deciphered control under one fixed metric.** Sign-level LZ `Δ` is **−10.12%** for Indus and **−11.57%** for Linear B, a **1.45 percentage-point** gap. Linear A sign-level `Δ` is **−4.76%**; Voynich gives word-level **−0.37%** and letter-level **−17.72%**. **[interpretation]** One Linear B control is a point, not a “deciphered-language band.” **Evidence:** [TRIFECTA_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md](artifacts/trifecta/TRIFECTA_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md) §§2–4 and [trifecta_structure_results.json](artifacts/trifecta/trifecta_structure_results.json).

- **[measured] Four of five Voynich probes reject their stated scribble/anagram nulls.** V1–V4 have z values **+398.3, −321.6, +205.3, +81.7**; V5 word-order compressibility is **−0.369%**, `|z|=2.98`, below the pre-set `3.0` threshold. **[interpretation]** The tested i.i.d. scribble model is inadequate; this does not establish language, cipher, or meaning. **Evidence:** [VOYNICH_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md](artifacts/trifecta/VOYNICH_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md) and [voynich_structure_results.json](artifacts/trifecta/voynich_structure_results.json).

- **[measured] The pre-registered FlyWire slice separates from both structural control ensembles on all four registered metrics.** The binary directed slice has **13,550 nodes** and **55,558 arcs**. Observed reciprocity, 3-cycle rate, feed-forward rate, and LZ ratio are respectively **0.201699**, **0.138216**, **0.715289**, and **0.504333**, versus degree-preserving-control means **0.006401**, **0.028352**, **0.118942**, and **0.536969**. **[interpretation]** This is graph structure in one external challenge-distribution slice, not cognition, function, or a quantum result. **Evidence:** [FlyWire REPORT.md](external_data/flywire/REPORT.md), [PREREGISTRATION.md](external_data/flywire/PREREGISTRATION.md), and [RESULTS.json](external_data/flywire/RESULTS.json).

## Open

- **[open; measured boundary] No end-to-end AI quality gain has been demonstrated.** There is no measured replacement for transformer attention and no end-to-end improvement in text generation, classification, reasoning, calibration, or safety. **Evidence:** [QUANTUM_AI_TRAINING_IDEAS.md](QUANTUM_AI_TRAINING_IDEAS.md) §§1–2, 6.

- **[open; measured boundary] Rung 2 needs a continuous rugged objective on which GD actually applies.** Rung 1 only establishes losses to GD on T0/T1 and a trainer win where GD was N/A; its fixed budget was **800** evaluations. **Evidence:** [BRAID_VS_GD_BENCHMARK.md](artifacts/braid_vs_gd/BRAID_VS_GD_BENCHMARK.md) §§2–6.

- **[open; measured boundary] The qubit-cascade proof needs a closure test.** The correction must reproduce `reconstruct()` to machine precision or analytically bound the residual; the documented present mismatch is MSE **≈1.6–2.1**. **Evidence:** [TN_CAPSTONE_REPORT.md](validation/tn_capstone/TN_CAPSTONE_REPORT.md) §5.4.

- **[open; measured boundary] Indus needs a second deciphered control and a larger corpus.** The current “control” comparison is **one** Linear B point, and the committed Indus corpus is only **179** Mohenjo-daro sides; full-corpus claims await a digitized Mahadevan corpus. **Evidence:** [TRIFECTA_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md](artifacts/trifecta/TRIFECTA_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md) §3 and [INDUS_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md](artifacts/trifecta/INDUS_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md) §4.

- **[open; measured boundary] Voynich phonetic reading and Linear A decipherment remain impossible from these data.** The study has **0** bilinguals for those scripts and makes no sign-value assignment. **Evidence:** [VOYNICH_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md](artifacts/trifecta/VOYNICH_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md) §4 and [LINEARA_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md](artifacts/trifecta/LINEARA_STRUCTURE_PROBE.md) §1.

- **[open; measured boundary] Higher-dimensional no-LHV results and d=4 MES control recovery are search-limited.** No FBSC `d=3,4` violation was found, and the d=4 MES optimizer did not recover the known analytic optimum. **Evidence:** [QUDIT_LHV_REPORT.md](artifacts/quantum_likeness/QUDIT_LHV_REPORT.md) §§0, 3.

- **[open; measured boundary] Real EEG and biological resonance have not been measured.** The validated tone tests used **4 s at 250 Hz** and do not validate 41.02 Hz as biological resonance. **Evidence:** [F0_SPECTRAL_RADIUS_UNIFICATION.md](resonance_fix/F0_SPECTRAL_RADIUS_UNIFICATION.md) §§5–6.

Their success/failure rules and baselines are deliberately pre-registered in [QUANTUM_AI_TRAINING_IDEAS.md](QUANTUM_AI_TRAINING_IDEAS.md) §§3–5. **Exact current result count: 0 end-to-end AI wins.**

It is falsified if sequence/current context alone predicts outcomes perfectly, or if monolithic end-to-end models consistently generalize better. **Exact benchmark result count: 0.** **Evidence:** [scientific_framing.md](egf/scientific_framing.md) §§1–4.

The document specifies metrics but records no result artifact. **Evidence:** [negative_information_experiment.md](negative_information_experiment.md) §§2–6.

- **[speculation] Ghost-in-the-Machine stress-test extensions:** the computational design uses a **72-crossing** braid, a **100-neuron** 80E/20I receiver, noise `η=0.0…0.99`, and **8** seeded trials per condition per noise level.
