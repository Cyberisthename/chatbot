## Honest scope (the lines we hold)

- Simulation-only. No hardware, no anyons physically realized, no loophole-free experiments.
  "Exact classical simulation" is the ceiling.
- No AGI / sentience / consciousness claims. F0 is a frequency; `is_sentient` is a measurement
  alias.
- Not a universal quantum computer: ≤3-parameter family is measure-zero (cannot represent
  generic states) — MSE=0 means exactness against the generator's own closed form.
- Nitrogenase/FeMo-co: one blind-prediction data point, not chemistry validation.
- Undeciphered scripts: structure ≠ meaning; the trifecta numbers are documented
  (`docs/artifacts/factcheck/FACTCHECK.md`) but are **not** in this release's reproducibility
  table (analysis scripts were destroyed; raw corpora not in-repo). Indus method + corpus stay
  committed (`docs/artifacts/indus/`).
- Legacy root scripts sit outside the owned core: `train_jarvis.py` / `train_llm.py` /
  `convert_to_gguf.py` (torch + transformers) and `jarvis_historical_training_pipeline.py` /
  `jarvis_v1_quantum_oracle_train.py` (HuggingFace `datasets`, auto-installed at runtime).
  They are **being relocated to `legacy/`** with a boundary README (owner-decided 2026-10-26, in
  flight) — until that lands they remain at the repo root, so this release does not claim a clean
  tree. Boundary doc: `docs/artifacts/legal/LEGACY_DEPENDENCIES_NOTE.md` (as committed it documents
  the three torch/transformers root importers; its §4 owner decision was *pending* at its
  2026-10-05 date and will be updated with the relocation). The legacy `*_MISSION_COMPLETE.md`
  completion claims (947GB-dataset training, Hugging Face deployment) are legacy claims outside the
  owned core and are **NOT used as evidence in this release**.
- Known issue disclosed: `ERROR_BOUND_PROOF.md` Appendix A (qubit path as 2×2 braid unitaries)
  does not describe `_reconstruct_qubit`, whose 2-level mixing is non-unitary (columns unit-norm,
  non-orthogonal; inner product −2cosθ·sinθ·sinφ ≠ 0) with a final normalization the doc's reading
  omits. The measured results stand (rows 1–3 reproduce exactly); a literal all-unitary reading
  gives normalized MSE 1.58/2.12/1.85 at n=8/32/256, while the closed form *with* normalization
  matches `reconstruct()` at ~8e-32 (capstone smoke R1b). Proof-doc amendment in progress
  (`reconstruct()` unchanged); see README "Known issue found after release".
- Branding: the historical "J.A.R.V.I.S." name is a Marvel/Disney trademark; renaming decision
  is pending owner sign-off — this release refers to the owned quantum-inspired core / FBSC.
